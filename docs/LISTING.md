## Description (paste, then add the GIFs)

> **Give every player a gem. Make them fight to keep it.**
>
> Facet Gems adds eight elemental gems to your SMP. Each has one active ability (right-click) and passive effects while carried. Climb four tiers with shards, steal gems from the players you beat, and race the whole server for the limited top tier.
>
> **The eight gems**
> - Ruby - *Rampage*: Strength, Haste and Resistance burst
> - Topaz - *Windstep*: dash that damages everyone in your path
> - Emerald - *Lifebloom*: heal you and your team, plus a shield
> - Sunstone - *Inferno*: fire burst that burns and knocks back (never sets blocks alight)
> - Onyx - *Gravity Well*: pull enemies where you look, then slam them
> - Sapphire - *Deep Freeze*: freeze and launch nearby enemies; no fall damage
> - Citrine - *Fortune's Favour*: Hero of the Village burst, extra XP from everything
> - Amethyst - *Thunderstrike*: lightning on the nearest enemies, charges creepers
>
> **Why server owners pick it**
> - **Dupe-proof by design.** Every gem has a unique ID and version in a ledger. Clones, creative copies and rollback copies are detected and removed automatically.
> - **Can't be lost by accident.** Bound gems can't be dropped or stored (chests, shulkers, bundles, ender chests, frames, hoppers, crafting grids) and can't burn, blow up or despawn on the ground. `/gem recover` replaces a lost gem and kills every other copy.
> - **Real PvP stakes.** Gems drop as loot on death (respects keepInventory). Kills pay shards, with a per-victim anti-farm limit.
> - **Plays nice with your server.** Abilities use normal damage events, so claims, regions and spawn protection still apply. Never hits villagers or pets. Scoreboard teams are allies.
> - **Themes.** Same powers, any concept. Ships with *Elements* and *Condiments*; make your own by editing names and colours - no code.
> - **Every number configurable.** Cooldowns, damage, radii, tiers, costs, drop chances, the server cap. `/gem reload` lists any mistakes; bad values are clamped, never a crash.
> - **Clean.** No telemetry, no licence checks, no internet access, no obfuscated surprises. Optional textures included.
>
> **Tested on** Paper 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 (Java 25) - every build is booted on all three automatically.
>
> **Support:** run `/gem debug`, paste the output in our Discord / a conversation. Documentation is included in the download.
